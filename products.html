Product 02 · Data AI  ·  New

Sovereign Data Platform

Govern your data. Keep your packets sealed. A data-centric platform built on the 5G core you already own — its classifier is an expansion of the core's own policy control and data-division functions. It sorts every flow by policy, never by reading the payload, and routes sensitive data to AI inside your perimeter: ours or your own. Everything else can reach the cloud. Same behavior over 5G, Wi-Fi, or wired.

Platform features

  • Classifier = core expansionPolicy control (extended PCF) authors data-class policy; data division (UPF steering) enforces it as PCC/PDR rules. Not a bolt-on box.
  • Classified by policy, never by payloadDecisions use slice (S-NSSAI), DNN/APN, UE group and application ID — the contents of your data are never opened.
  • Tunable, versioned policyRefine data-class rules per site, slice or group; tighten residency without touching the access network.
  • Access-independentGoverns data the same way over 5G, Wi-Fi or wired — no dependency on a specific radio.
  • Compliance evidence by designRouting and inference access are logged as a by-product — usable for EU AI Act, HIPAA and data-residency review.

Camera intelligence — person, intrusion and zone-violation detection — processed at the on-site edge over your private 5G. A control point that decides where every AI request runs — on-prem or cloud — by how sensitive the data is. Metadata-driven steering that works on encrypted traffic, central policy with a full audit trail, and a single egress gate you control: the only door out of the sovereign boundary.
