Why full control of the core

Security is an architecture, not a setting.

Most private 5G is stitched together from vendors who each touch your traffic, or it's backhauled to a public cloud — either way, your data leaves your control. We build on a hardened open-source core and layer our own customized AI and orchestration software on top, so no proprietary vendor sits in your signaling or data path, and nothing sensitive has to leave the building.

How it fits together

One perimeter. Four products. Nothing sensitive crosses the line.

The core steers and protects traffic; AI runs at the edge and in the management plane. The 5G core routes camera streams but never inspects their contents — video is decrypted and analyzed only at the on-site edge. Raw footage and subscriber data stay inside the perimeter, and only policy-approved metadata reaches the cloud, and only if you allow it.

The products

Four products on one sovereign platform.

Product 01 · Foundation

Sovereign 5G Core

A converged private 4G/5G core built on a hardened open-source foundation that you run and control end to end — engineered for sites where data sovereignty is the requirement, not a feature.

Features

  • Built on a hardened open-source core you can audit end to end — transparency that closed, black-box stacks can't offer
  • Full control of every network function — no vendor locked into your control or data path
  • Runs on-premises or fully air-gapped; you hold the keys and the subscriber identities
  • Accelerated user plane with local breakout — traffic stays on site, never backhauled
  • Per-tenant network slicing with isolated QoS and security domains
  • Cameras, sensors, and devices authenticated as managed devices on your network

Use cases

  • Semiconductor fabs and production lines that can't export process data
  • Hospitals operating under strict patient-data regulation
  • Defense and government sites that need a self-contained network
Product 02 · Operations

NetOps AI

An AI operations assistant that lives inside your core — it diagnoses faults, explains them against the 3GPP standard, and proposes fixes, without sending a single log off site.

Features

  • Root-cause analysis grounded in your own logs, traces, and metrics — every answer cited, never guessed
  • Plain-language explanations of signaling failures tied to the exact standard procedure
  • Generates and validates core configuration against 3GPP specifications
  • Closed-loop automation with human approval, staged rollout, and automatic rollback
  • Runs on-premises or air-gapped — your operational data never leaves the building

Use cases

  • Lean teams running private networks without a 24/7 operations center
  • Integrators and managed-service providers running many sites from one console
  • Regulated operators that can't use cloud-hosted AI ops tools
Product 03 · Vision

Sovereign Edge Vision

Camera intelligence — intrusion and anomaly detection — processed at the edge over your private 5G. The video never leaves the premises, and the core never sees its contents.

Features

  • Person, intrusion, and zone-violation detection at the on-site edge using modern vision models
  • Natural-language alerts and footage search — “person in a restricted zone after hours”
  • Video steered to the on-site edge — never backhauled, never sent to a cloud
  • The core routes and prioritizes streams but never inspects content — privacy by architecture
  • The network reacts to detections automatically — prioritizing the stream that just saw motion

Use cases

  • Perimeter and restricted-zone monitoring at fabs, depots, and critical infrastructure
  • After-hours intrusion detection across multi-building campuses
  • Retail and franchise chains needing private-wireless cameras under central control
Product 04 · Governance

Policy-Driven AI Gateway

A control point that decides where every AI request runs — on-premises or cloud — based on how sensitive the data is. Regulated data stays inside; only what's permitted goes out.

Features

  • Routes AI and inference requests by data classification, slice, and tenant policy
  • Keeps sensitive prompts and data on-premises; bursts only non-sensitive work to the cloud
  • Metadata-driven steering that works on encrypted traffic — no payload inspection required
  • Central policy with a full audit trail of what crossed the perimeter, and why
  • A single egress gate you control — the only door out of the sovereign boundary

Use cases

  • Regulated industries adopting AI under data-residency and AI-governance rules
  • Mixed workloads where some data can use cloud AI and some legally cannot
  • Multi-tenant sites needing per-tenant AI data-handling policies
