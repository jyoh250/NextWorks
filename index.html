Why sovereign AI

Security is an architecture, not a setting.

Most private 5G is stitched from vendors who each touch your traffic, or it's backhauled to a public cloud — either way your data leaves your control. We build on a hardened open-source core and layer our own AI and orchestration on top, so no proprietary vendor sits in your signaling or data path, and nothing sensitive has to leave the building.

Product 02 · Data AI New

Sovereign Data Platform

A data-centric platform built on the 5G core you own. Its classifier extends the core's own policy control and data-division functions — sorting every flow by policy, never by reading the payload — and routes sensitive data to AI inside your perimeter: ours or your own. Everything else can reach the cloud.

What it does

  • Classifier = core expansionExtends the PCF (policy) and UPF steering (data division) — not a bolt-on box.
  • No payload disclosureSorts by slice, DNN/APN, UE group and app ID — never the contents of your data.
  • Provided or your own AIRun our managed on-prem AI, or connect the AI you already operate.
